Best Places in Sirohi

Yogeshwar Dham

Yogeshwar Dham, located within the campus of SP College, Sirohi, is a peaceful and spiritually uplifting place. Surrounded by a calm environment, it offers a space for meditation, devotion, and self-reflection. The Dham reflects Indian spiritual values and provides positive energy to everyone who visits, making it a place of faith, serenity, and inner strength.

Mount Abu

Mount Abu is a popular hill station located in the Aravalli Range of Rajasthan, India. It is known for its cool climate, scenic views, and lush green surroundings. Mount Abu is famous for attractions like Dilwara Temples, Nakki Lake, and Sunset Point. It is a peaceful tourist destination and a perfect escape from the desert heat of Rajasthan.

Explore

Dilwara Jain Temple

The Dilwara Jain Temples are a group of beautiful Jain temples located in Mount Abu, Rajasthan. They are famous for their stunning marble carvings and detailed architecture. These temples were built between the 11th and 13th centuries. The Dilwara Temples are considered one of the finest examples of Jain art and craftsmanship in India.

Explore

Achalgarh Fort

Achalgarh Fort is an ancient fort located near Mount Abu in Rajasthan. It was originally built in the 11th century and later renovated by Maharana Kumbha. The fort is known for its strong walls and historical importance. It also offers a beautiful view of the surrounding hills and landscape.

About Sirohi

Sirohi is a picturesque district located in the southwestern part of Rajasthan, India. Known for its rich heritage, scenic Aravalli hills, and historic architecture, Sirohi offers travelers a perfect blend of nature, culture, and history.

The region is famous for the hill station of Mount Abu, the serene Dilwara Jain Temples, and historic forts like Achalgarh. Visitors can explore beautiful landscapes, enjoy trekking trails, and immerse themselves in the local Rajasthani culture.

Sirohi is also known for its traditional handicrafts, vibrant festivals, and warm hospitality. Whether you are a history enthusiast, nature lover, or spiritual seeker, Sirohi has something special to offer.
