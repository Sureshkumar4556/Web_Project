Mount Abu

The only hill station in Rajasthan, known for its cool climate, green landscapes, and beautiful temples.

About Mount Abu

Mount Abu is located in the Aravalli Range and is Rajasthan's only hill station. Surrounded by lush green hills and serene lakes, it offers a refreshing escape from the desert heat. Famous for the Dilwara Jain Temples, Nakki Lake, and sunset points, Mount Abu attracts tourists year-round.

Activities include trekking, boating, sightseeing, and exploring the rich cultural heritage. The town is also known for its traditional Rajasthani cuisine and handicrafts.
